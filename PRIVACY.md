# Car Dash – privacy policy

Car Dash is a personal, non-commercial app that shows live engine data from an OBD-II
Bluetooth adapter plugged into your own car.

- **No data is collected by the developer.** The app has no account, no analytics, no ads
  and no internet access.
- **Everything stays on your phone.** Readings, drive recordings, fuel and battery history
  and car settings are stored in the app's private storage.
- **Sharing is only by you.** Files leave the phone only when you tap a SHARE button and
  choose where to send them.
- **Bluetooth** ("Nearby devices") is used only to talk to the OBD adapter you choose.
- **Your car:** the app only sends standard read requests (OBD-II services 01, 03, 07, 09);
  it never changes or clears anything in the car.

Uninstalling the app deletes all its data.

Contact: through the GitHub repository github.com/Arka01/car-dash-android (Issues).
